## Method

An approved refund goes back to the original payment method.

<!-- fact id=refund.method | include=original payment method | length=short | aliases=original payment method -->

## Partial

A partial refund never exceeds the captured amount.

<!-- fact id=refund.partial | include=never exceeds the captured amount | length=short | aliases=partial refund captured -->

## Window

The refund window for a standard refund is 30 days from approval.
