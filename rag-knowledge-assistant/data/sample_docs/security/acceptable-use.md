# Acceptable Use Policy (SEC-POL-001)

## Company Devices

Company devices are for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work or violate policy.

## Prohibited Activities

Installing unapproved software, disabling endpoint protection, and sharing accounts are prohibited.

## Monitoring

Company devices and accounts may be monitored for security purposes in accordance with local law.
