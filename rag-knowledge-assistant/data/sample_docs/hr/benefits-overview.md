# US Health Benefits Overview (HR-REF-300)

## Medical, Dental and Vision

Lumen Analytics covers 90% of medical premiums for employees and 75% for dependents. Dental and vision plans are fully covered for employees.

## Enrollment

Benefits elections are made within 30 days of the start date or during open enrollment in November.

## Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers 6 free confidential counseling sessions per issue per year.
