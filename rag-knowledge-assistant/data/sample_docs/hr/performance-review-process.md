# Performance Review Calibration Guide for Managers (HR-PRO-330)

This guide is confidential and intended for people managers and HR only.

## Rating Distribution Guideline

The "Exceptional" rating should be given to no more than 15% of a manager's direct reports. The "Needs Improvement" rating requires a documented performance plan.

## Timeline

Self-reviews open on November 1. Calibration sessions take place in the first two weeks of December. Ratings are shared with employees by January 15.

## Promotion Nominations

Promotion nominations must be submitted during calibration with a written case covering scope, impact and peer feedback.
