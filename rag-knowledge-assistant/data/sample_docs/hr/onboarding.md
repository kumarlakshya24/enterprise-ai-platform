# New Employee Onboarding (HR-PRO-101)

## First Day

New employees receive their laptop on the first day at the IT Depot or by courier for remote hires. Orientation starts at 09:30 local time.

## First 90 Days

The onboarding period lasts 90 days and includes mandatory security awareness training within the first 14 days.

## Onboarding Buddy

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 90 days.
