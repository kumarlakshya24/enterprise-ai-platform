# Parental Leave Policy (HR-POL-215)

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for parental leave after 6 months of continuous employment. The policy covers birth, adoption and foster placement.

## Leave Duration

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Phased Return

After parental leave, employees may use a 4-week phased return, working a 60% schedule at full pay.

## How to Apply

Notify your manager and submit a Leave of Absence case in Workday at least 30 days before the expected leave start date where possible.
