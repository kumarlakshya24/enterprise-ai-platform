# Learning and Development Budget (HR-POL-260)

## Annual Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books, certifications and conferences.

## Approval

Requests over $500 require manager approval before purchase. Submit learning expenses in Expensify under the "Learning" category.

## Conference Travel

Conference travel follows the Travel and Expense Policy (FIN-POL-310) and does not count against the learning budget.
