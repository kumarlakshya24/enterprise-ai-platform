# Employee Referral Program (HR-POL-270)

## Referral Bonus

Employees receive a $3,000 referral bonus when a referred candidate is hired, paid after the new hire completes 90 days of employment.

## How to Refer

Submit referrals through the Referrals tab in Workday Recruiting with the candidate's CV.

## Exclusions

Managers cannot receive a bonus for hiring into their own team, and rehires of former employees are excluded.
