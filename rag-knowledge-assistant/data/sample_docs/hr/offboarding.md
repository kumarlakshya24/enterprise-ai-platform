# Leaving the Company (HR-PRO-140)

## Notice Period

Employees are asked to give at least 2 weeks' notice in writing to their manager.

## Returning Equipment

Laptops, badges and company phones must be returned within 5 business days of the last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid return kit.

## Final Pay

Unused accrued PTO is paid out in the final paycheck where required by local law.
