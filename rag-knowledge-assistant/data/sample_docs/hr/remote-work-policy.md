# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (HR-POL-104)

This policy applies to all Lumen Analytics employees unless a team-specific addendum overrides a section. Team addenda (for example the Finance analyst addendum or the Field Sales policy) take precedence for the employees they cover.

## 1. Eligibility

1.1 All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period are eligible for hybrid work.

1.2 Employees in roles that require on-site equipment (for example the IT Depot and facilities staff) are not eligible for hybrid work.

## 2. Hybrid Schedule

2.1 Hybrid employees work from the office three anchor days per week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

2.2 Monday and Friday are flexible days that may be worked remotely without additional approval.

2.3 Managers may approve a temporary fully-remote arrangement of up to 4 consecutive weeks for medical or family reasons.

## 3. Working From Another Country

3.1 Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year.

3.2 Requests must be submitted at least 15 business days in advance using the Workday form RW-2 and require approval from both the manager and the Global Mobility team.

3.3 Working from a sanctioned country is never permitted.

## 4. Equipment and Stipends

4.1 Eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for furniture and peripherals.

4.2 A monthly internet stipend of $40 is paid through payroll.

4.3 Company laptops remain company property and must be returned within 5 business days of leaving the company.

## 5. Security Requirements

5.1 Employees working outside an office must connect through the GlobalProtect VPN before accessing internal systems.

5.2 Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN connected. Screens must be locked when unattended.
