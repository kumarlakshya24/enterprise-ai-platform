# Paid Time Off Policy (HR-POL-201)

## Accrual

Paid time off (PTO) accrues each pay period based on tenure:

| Tenure | Annual PTO days |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 2 years | 15 days |
| 2 to 5 years | 20 days |
| More than 5 years | 25 days |

## Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit PTO requests in Workday. Requests longer than 3 consecutive days require at least 2 weeks' notice.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not carry over.
