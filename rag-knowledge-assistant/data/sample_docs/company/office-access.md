# Office Access and Visitors (FAC-POL-012)

## Badges

Your employee badge opens all office entrances. Report a lost badge at the reception desk; a replacement is issued the same day.

## Visitors

Register visitors in Envoy at least 24 hours before their arrival. Visitors must be escorted at all times.

## After-Hours Access

Access to the office between 22:00 and 06:00 requires prior approval from the Security team.
