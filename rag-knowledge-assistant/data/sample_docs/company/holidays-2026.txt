COMPANY HOLIDAYS 2026

The following days are paid company holidays for all US employees:

New Year's Day - January 1
Martin Luther King Jr. Day - January 19
Memorial Day - May 25
Founders' Day - June 12
Independence Day - July 3 (observed)
Labor Day - September 7
Thanksgiving - November 26 and 27
Winter Break - December 24 to December 31

FLOATING HOLIDAYS
Each employee also receives 2 floating holidays per year, to be requested in Workday like PTO.
