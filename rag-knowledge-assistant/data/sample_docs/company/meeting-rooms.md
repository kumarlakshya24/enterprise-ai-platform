# Booking Meeting Rooms (FAC-KB-020)

## Booking

Book meeting rooms through Outlook by adding the room as an attendee. Rooms booked but unused for 15 minutes are released automatically.

## Large Rooms

Rooms for more than 12 people require booking through the Facilities team at least 3 days in advance.
