# Field Sales and Sales Operations Remote Work Policy (SAL-POL-022)

This policy covers Account Executives, Field Sales representatives and Sales Operations analysts. It replaces the hybrid schedule in HR-POL-104 for these roles.

## Remote-First Roles

Field Sales representatives and Sales Operations analysts are remote-first and are not required to work from an office on a weekly schedule.

## In-Person Requirements

Sales staff attend the in-person Quarterly Business Review (QBR) week in the regional hub office once per quarter. Travel for QBR week is booked through Navan and charged to the Sales cost center.

## Home Office

Field Sales representatives receive a home office stipend of $750 instead of the standard $500, plus a mobile phone allowance of $60 per month.

## Customer Data

Customer contracts must be stored in Salesforce, never on local laptop storage.
