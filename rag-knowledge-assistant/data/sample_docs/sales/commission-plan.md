# Sales Commission Plan 2026 (SAL-COMP-2026)

Internal to the Sales team.

## Commission Rate

Account Executives earn 10% commission on new annual recurring revenue (ARR).

## Accelerators

Commission on ARR above 100% of quota is paid at a 1.5x accelerator.

## Clawback

Commission is clawed back if the customer churns within 90 days of contract signature.
