# Passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication (IT-KB-002)

## Resetting Your Password

Reset a forgotten password yourself at id.lumen.internal using your registered Okta Verify device. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

IT staff will never ask for your password by email, chat or phone. Do not share your password with anyone, including people claiming to be from IT.

## Lost or New Phone

If you lose the phone with Okta Verify, call the Service Desk at extension 4357. After verifying your identity on a video call, the Service Desk issues a temporary bypass code valid for 12 hours so you can enroll a new device.

## MFA Requirements

MFA is required for all applications behind Okta, including email, Workday and Salesforce.
