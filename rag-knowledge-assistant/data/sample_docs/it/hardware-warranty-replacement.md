# Hardware Warranty and Replacement Policy (IT-POL-031)

## Warranty Coverage

All company laptops are covered by a 3-year Dell ProSupport Plus warranty, which includes next-business-day on-site repair.

## Accidental Damage

Accidental damage, such as liquid spills or drops, is covered once per device lifecycle at no cost to the employee. A second accidental damage incident on the same device requires manager approval and a $250 charge to the team's cost center.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the Security team within 24 hours using the Security Incident form. The device will be remotely wiped.

## Refresh Cycle

| Device type | Standard model | Refresh cycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard laptop | Dell Latitude 7450 | 36 months |
| Engineering laptop | MacBook Pro 14 M4 | 36 months |
| Design workstation | MacBook Pro 16 M4 Max | 30 months |
| Monitor | Dell U2724D | 60 months |

Devices older than their refresh cycle are replaced automatically; IT will contact you 30 days before your refresh date.
