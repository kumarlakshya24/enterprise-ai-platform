# Monitors, Docks and Peripherals (IT-KB-021)

## Standard Kit

Each employee can order one monitor, one docking station, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset from the IT catalog at no charge to their team.

## Additional Equipment

A second monitor requires manager approval, except for Finance analysts and Engineering staff, who receive it by default.

## Faulty Peripherals

Faulty peripherals are swapped at the IT Depot without a ticket. Remote employees should open a ServiceDesk ticket to receive a replacement by mail.
