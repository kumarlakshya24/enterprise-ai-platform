# Laptop Not Working - Troubleshooting and Support (IT-KB-017)

Use this guide if your company laptop will not power on, crashes repeatedly, or has a broken screen or keyboard.

## Step 1: Basic Troubleshooting

1. Connect the original charger and check that the charging LED lights up.
2. Perform a hard reset by holding the power button for 15 seconds, then press it again to start the laptop.
3. If the laptop starts but crashes, restart in Safe Mode and note any error message.

## Step 2: Contact the Service Desk

If the laptop still does not work, open a Priority 2 (P2) hardware ticket in the ServiceDesk portal at help.lumen.internal, or call the IT hotline at extension 4357 (HELP). From an external phone, call +1 800 555 0199.

## Step 3: Loaner Laptops

On-site employees can collect a loaner laptop from the IT Depot (Building B, floor 2) within 4 business hours of opening the ticket. Remote employees receive a loaner laptop by overnight shipping through DepotExpress.

## Your Data

Files in your Documents and Desktop folders are synced to OneDrive automatically, so they will be available on the loaner laptop after you sign in. Files stored elsewhere on the local disk may be lost.

## Repairs and Replacement

Repair, warranty and replacement decisions are covered in the Hardware Warranty and Replacement Policy (IT-POL-031).
