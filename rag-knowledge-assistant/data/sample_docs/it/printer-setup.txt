OFFICE PRINTER SETUP (IT-KB-033)

OVERVIEW
All office printers use PaperCut follow-me printing. Print jobs are held in a secure queue until you release them at any printer.

INSTALLING THE PRINTER
Install the "Lumen-FollowMe" printer from the Company Portal app. No driver download is needed.

RELEASING A PRINT JOB
Tap your badge on the printer's card reader to release your jobs. Jobs not released within 24 hours are deleted.
