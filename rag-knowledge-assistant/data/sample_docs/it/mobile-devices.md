# Mobile Phones and BYOD (IT-POL-036)

## Bring Your Own Device

Personal phones may access email and Slack only after enrolling in Intune app protection. Full device management is not required for personal phones.

## Company Phones

Company phones are issued to on-call engineers and field sales staff. A broken company phone is replaced through the ServiceDesk portal within 2 business days.

## Lost Phones

Report a lost company phone to the Service Desk immediately so it can be locked and wiped remotely.
