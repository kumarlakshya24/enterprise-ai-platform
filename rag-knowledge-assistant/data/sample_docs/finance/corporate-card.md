# Corporate Card Policy (FIN-POL-315)

## Eligibility

Employees who travel more than four times per year can request an Amex corporate card through the Finance portal with manager approval.

## Spending Limits

The standard monthly limit is $5,000. Personal purchases on the corporate card are prohibited, even if repaid.

## Reconciliation

Card transactions must be reconciled in Expensify by the 5th of the following month. Cards with transactions unreconciled for 60 days are suspended.
