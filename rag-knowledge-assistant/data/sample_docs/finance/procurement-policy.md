# Procurement and Purchasing Policy (FIN-POL-320)

## Approval Thresholds

| Purchase amount | Required approval | Competitive bids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under $5,000 | Line manager | Not required |
| $5,000 to $50,000 | Department head and Procurement | 2 quotes |
| Over $50,000 | CFO | 3 competitive bids |

## Vendor Onboarding

Any vendor that will handle customer data must pass a security review (the vendor security questionnaire) before a contract is signed.

## Purchase Orders

A purchase order must be issued before any work starts. Invoices without a matching purchase order will not be paid.
