# Travel and Expense Policy (FIN-POL-310)

## Submitting Expenses

Submit expense reports in Expensify within 30 days of the expense date. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25.

## Meal Per Diem

| City tier | Example cities | Daily meal per diem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | New York, London, San Francisco | $95 |
| Tier 2 | Chicago, Berlin, Toronto | $75 |
| Tier 3 | All other cities | $60 |

## Flights

Book flights through Navan. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at $300 per night in Tier 1 cities and $200 per night elsewhere.
