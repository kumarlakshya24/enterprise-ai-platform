# Finance Analyst Remote Work Addendum (FIN-ADD-104A)

This addendum modifies HR-POL-104 for Financial Analysts, Senior Financial Analysts and FP&A Analysts in the Finance team. It does not apply to analysts in other departments.

## Remote Allowance

Finance analysts may work remotely up to 4 days per week, with one in-office day chosen together with their manager.

## Month-End Close Requirements

Finance analysts must work on-site during month-end close, on business days 1 through 3 of each month. Exceptions require written approval from the Controller.

## Systems Access

SOX-controlled systems, including the general ledger, may only be accessed from the Citrix Finance Desktop (VDI) when working remotely. Downloading ledger exports to personal devices is prohibited.

## Equipment

Finance analysts receive a second monitor in addition to the standard home office stipend.
