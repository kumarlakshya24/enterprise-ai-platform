# Month-End Close Procedure (FIN-PRO-110)

Internal to the Finance team.

## Close Calendar

The close runs from business day 1 (BD1) to business day 5 (BD5). The journal entry cutoff is BD2 at 17:00 Eastern Time. Late entries require Controller approval.

## Reconciliations

All balance sheet account reconciliations must be completed and reviewed by BD4.

## Flash Report

The preliminary revenue flash report is sent to the CFO on BD3.
