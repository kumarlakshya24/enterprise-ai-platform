# Payroll Schedule and Payslips (FIN-REF-201)

## Pay Dates

US employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of each month. If a pay date falls on a weekend, payment is made on the preceding Friday.

## Payslips

Payslips are available in Workday under Pay > Payslips two days before each pay date.

## Payroll Errors

Report payroll errors to payroll@lumen-analytics.com. Corrections are processed in the next pay cycle, or within 3 business days for underpayments over $200.
